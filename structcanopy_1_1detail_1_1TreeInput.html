◆ min_uniform_depth

int canopy::detail::TreeInput::min_uniform_depth = 0

Force BFS to refine every panel to at least this depth before the per-panel tolerance test is allowed to mark a node as a leaf. Useful for driving the leaf-table fast path: a uniformly-refined tree of depth D in input_dim K builds a 2^(K*D)-entry quantize table at construction (see PolyTree::leaf_table_), turning the eval-time leaf lookup into one SIMD quantize + one u32 load. Default 0 (no forcing — tol-based refinement only).
